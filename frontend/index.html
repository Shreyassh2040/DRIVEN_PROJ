About CivicFlow

A calmer, more accountable way to run city services.

CivicFlow is the Smart City Assistance and Discovery Platform: a single place where residents discover municipal services, report civic issues and participate in local decisions.

Every complaint is tracked with a permanent ID, status history and assigned officer. Every poll enforces one vote per citizen. Every administrative action is recorded in an activity log.

Accountable by design

Role-based access, audit logging and server-side validation protect the integrity of every record.

Transparent where it matters

Citizens see the public history of their complaint. Internal officer notes stay internal.

Grounded in real records

Service availability, complaint counts and poll results come from the city database — never invented for display.

Built for participation

Public registration creates citizen accounts only. Officer and administrator access is provisioned by the city.
